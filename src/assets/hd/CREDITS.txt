HD houses (house looks per area):
Street imagery attributes derived from Mapillary (CC BY-SA)   https://www.mapillary.com   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  src/data/durban-house-styles.json: wall colours/finish, roof type and colour, boundary walls and fences per area,
  hand-tagged from Mapillary street photos (2017-2020) of residential streets; photo ids listed per area. No photos are stored.
  Sampling script: scripts/fetch-house-mapillary.py
Fence / boundary wall atlas and barred-window house facades: drawn procedurally in src/world/fences.js and src/world/facades.js (CC0, this project)

Bus / taxi stop shelters (src/world/shelters.js):
src/assets/props/bus-shelter.glb   "Bus Stop Shelter" by Grab3D, CC0 1.0 (public domain)
  https://grab3d.com/models/city/bus-stop-shelter/   https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  Recoloured to the blue of eThekwini municipal shelters, textures cut to 512 px, bolts/fittings dropped and
  simplified to ~2k triangles by scripts/optimize-shelter.mjs.
Shelter spots: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) - highway=bus_stop + shelter=yes, amenity=shelter +
  shelter_type=public_transport; two Phoenix-area stops confirmed on Mapillary street photos (CC BY-SA);
  built by scripts/add-shelters.py into src/data/durban-shelters.json. No photos are stored.
