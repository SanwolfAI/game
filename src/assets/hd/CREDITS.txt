BMW E30 interior (src/game/cockpit.js)
  All geometry and textures (instrument cluster, centre stack, sport cloth,
  headliner, roundel without lettering) are procedural, drawn in code for this
  game. Layout taken from the E30 owner's manual and parts catalogues; no
  photos are copied into the game.

Bus / taxi stop shelters (src/world/shelters.js): both shelter models (the eThekwini municipal dark-blue box
  shelter and the People Mover glass shelter) are built in code from boxes, shaped and coloured from Mapillary
  street photos of real Durban stops (CC0, this project). No third-party model is used.
Shelter spots: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) - highway=bus_stop + shelter=yes, amenity=shelter +
  shelter_type=public_transport, each checked against the nearest Mapillary street photos (CC BY-SA); stops
  added or removed on that evidence are listed with their Mapillary image ids in scripts/add-shelters.py,
  which builds src/data/durban-shelters.json. No photos are stored.

Gusheshe engine sound (src/assets/audio/e30_*.ogg / .m4a, built by scripts/build-engine-sound.py):
No free-licence recording of a real E30 325i (M20 straight-six) could be found, so these are the closest
BMW straight-six recordings with a licence that allows commercial use (six-cylinder firing pattern
checked from the recordings' sound; the models are as their uploaders name them).
